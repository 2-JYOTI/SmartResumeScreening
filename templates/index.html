Questions, answered.

Is Smart Resume Screening free?

Yes. You can run your first analysis for free with no credit card. Create a free account when you want to save reports or manage candidate history.

What does the analysis include?

You’ll receive a role-match score, matching skills, potential gaps, and practical suggestions for what to explore during the interview.

What happens to the resumes I upload?

Your files are used only to generate the requested analysis. See our Privacy Policy for details about data retention and deletion.

Will the tool make hiring decisions for me?

No. It is designed to support—not replace—recruiter judgment and structured hiring processes.

What file types can I upload?

Currently, upload text-based PDF resumes up to 5 MB.
